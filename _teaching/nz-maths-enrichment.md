A collection of talk slides and resources developed between 2021 and 2024 for New Zealand secondary-school students, introducing mathematics beyond the standard curriculum. Spanning topics such as topology, the Fibonacci numbers, and infinite series, the material aims to make mathematics accessible and fun — giving students a taste of the kind of mathematical thinking they don't usually meet at school.
