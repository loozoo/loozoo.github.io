Course Assistant for Prof. Oliver Knill's *Differential Geometry*. Content delivered includes of curves and surfaces, curvature, the Frenet–Serret and Gauss–Bonnet theorems, the Theorema Egregium, geodesics, and Riemannian manifolds with their application to general relativity and Einstein equations. Course materials can be found [here](https://people.math.harvard.edu/~knill/teaching/math136_2025/).
