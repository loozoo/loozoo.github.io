Course Assistant for Prof. Thomas Brazelton's *Sets, Groups and Geometry*. Content delivered includes  elementary set theory and logic, methods of proof, modular arithmetic and induction, group theory, and a geometric capstone on the Platonic and Archimedean solids via Euler's formula. Course notes can be found [here](https://tbrazel.github.io/assets/coursenotes/math101.pdf).
