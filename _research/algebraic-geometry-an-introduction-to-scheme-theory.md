A set of expository lecture notes developing the foundations of modern algebraic geometry, adapted from Harvard's Math 232ar in Fall 2025. Beginning with some essential category theory (universal properties, direct limits, the Yoneda lemma etc.), the notes go through the classical theory of algebraic sets and the Nullstellensatz to the spectrum of a ring, sheaves, and schemes. Later sections also treat projective schemes, the Proj construction, fibre products, and the central properties of schemes and their morphisms (separatedness, properness, smoothness, and the valuative criteria).
