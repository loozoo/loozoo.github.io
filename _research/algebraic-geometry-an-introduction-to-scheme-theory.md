A set of expository lecture notes developing the foundations of modern algebraic geometry, adapted from Harvard's Math 232ar in Fall 2025. Beginning with the categorical language of universal properties, limits, and the Yoneda lemma, the notes build through the classical theory of algebraic sets and the Nullstellensatz to the spectrum of a ring, sheaves, and the definition of a scheme. Later sections treat projective schemes and the Proj construction, fibered products, and the central properties of schemes and their morphisms — separatedness, properness, smoothness, and the valuative criteria.
