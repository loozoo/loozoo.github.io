title: "Affine Group Schemes in Relative Algebraic Geometry"

Classically, there is an equivalence between affine schemes over \\( R \\) and commutative \\( R \\)-algebras where \\( \mathrm{Aff}\_R \simeq (\mathrm{CAlg}\_R)^{\mathrm{op}} \\). Relative algebraic geometry generalizes this by working relative to a symmetric monoidal category \\( \mathcal{C} \\), where the affine objects are defined to be \\( \mathrm{Aff}\_{\mathcal{C}} := \mathrm{Comm}(\mathcal{C})^{\mathrm{op}} \\), the opposite of the category of commutative monoids ("rings") in \\( \mathcal{C} \\). Following the theory developed by Toën–Vaquié, this project studies how affine group schemes and commutative Hopf algebras behave in this generality.
