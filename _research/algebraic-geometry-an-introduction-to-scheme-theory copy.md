A set of expository lecture notes on computational algebraic topology and its use in data analysis, adapted from Oxford's C3.9 (Computational Algebraic Topology), taught by Professor Vidit Nanda. Beginning with simplicial complexes and simplicial homology, the notes develop persistent homology and the structure theorem for persistence modules, the matrix-reduction algorithm that computes barcodes, and the stability theorem guaranteeing robustness to noise. Later sections treat discrete Morse theory and the passage from these algebraic invariants to the analysis of real data.
