Quantum stabilizer codes are a class of quantum error-correcting codes defined by an abelian group of commuting Pauli operators with common \\(+1\\) eigenspace. When the code is translation-invariant on a lattice, this data admits a purely algebraic description: the (projectivised) Pauli operators form a free module over the ring of Laurent polynomials \\(R = \mathbb{F}_2[x_1^{\pm 1}, \dots, x_D^{\pm 1}]\\), under which the stabilizer group becomes a submodule, with commutation relations recorded by an \\(R\\)-valued symplectic form. This thesis develops the theory that emerges from this description, using the commutative and homological algebra of these modules to give physical interpretations for the corresponding quantum error-correcting codes.
