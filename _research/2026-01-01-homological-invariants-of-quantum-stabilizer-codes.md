Quantum stabilizer codes are a class of quantum error-correcting codes defined by an abelian group of commuting Pauli operators, whose common eigenspace encodes protected logical information. When the code is translation-invariant on a lattice, this data admits a purely algebraic description: the Pauli operators form a free module over the ring of Laurent polynomials \\(R = \mathbb{F}_2[x_1^{\pm 1}, \dots, x_D^{\pm 1}]\\), allowing the stabilizer group to be a submodule, and their commutation relations are recorded by an \\(R\\)-valued symplectic form. This thesis develops the theory that emerges from this description, using the commutative and homological algebra of these modules to give physical interpretations for the associated quantum error-correcting codes.
